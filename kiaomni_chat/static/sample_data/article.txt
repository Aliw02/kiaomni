The 2025 Frontier LLM Benchmark: DeepSeek-R1, Qwen2.5-Max, and KV-Cache Scaling

Boca Raton & Hangzhou Research Report — Published February 14, 2025

Between December 2024 and February 2025, the open-weights AI ecosystem experienced a massive shift with the releases of DeepSeek-V3, DeepSeek-R1, Llama 3.3 70B, and Alibaba's Qwen2.5-Max. This technical report summarizes the architectural specifications, benchmark scores, and KV-cache memory scaling properties evaluated across the 2025 frontier models.

Section 1: Open Reasoning Models and Distillation (DeepSeek-R1, January 20, 2025)

On January 20, 2025, DeepSeek AI released DeepSeek-R1, a 671-billion parameter Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) reasoning model trained via large-scale Reinforcement Learning (RL) without supervised fine-tuning (SFT) first. DeepSeek-R1 achieved a score of 79.8% on AIME 2024 and 90.8% on MATH-500. More importantly, DeepSeek distilled its reasoning capabilities into smaller dense models (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-14B and 32B), allowing 14B models to beat OpenAI's o1-mini on competitive programming benchmarks (Codeforces rating: 2020).

Section 2: Long-Context Frontier Scaling (Qwen2.5-Max, January 28, 2025)

On January 28, 2025, Alibaba Cloud introduced Qwen2.5-Max, a 1-million-token context model utilizing Multi-Head Latent Attention (MLA). On the Needle-In-A-Haystack (NIAH) 1M test, Qwen2.5-Max achieved 99.7% retrieval accuracy. However, holding KV tensors for a 1M token sequence at batch size 1 requires 256 GB of FP16 memory, forcing cloud providers to deploy 8xH100 GPU clusters per instance.

Section 3: KV-Cache Compression Breakthrough (KiaOmni-v2, February 10, 2025)

On February 10, 2025, researchers introduced KiaOmni-v2, a spatial-saliency eviction framework using continuous Gaussian smoothing (sigma=4) and observation window max-pooling (W=32). On the 128K context evaluation, KiaOmni-v2 reduced KV-cache memory from 32 GB down to 3.15 GB (an 89.8% compression ratio) while retaining 96.4% of Full-Context accuracy on multi-turn reasoning benchmarks, outperforming SnapKV's 71.2% accuracy.

Section 4: Hardware Specifications and Edge Deployment Benchmarks

The February 2025 benchmark matrix evaluated four edge deployment setups:
1. Setup A (NVIDIA RTX 4090 24GB): Ran DeepSeek-R1-Distill-14B + KiaOmni-v2 at 42.5 tokens/sec.
2. Setup B (Apple M3 Max 128GB): Ran Qwen2.5-32B at 18.2 tokens/sec.
3. Setup C (NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin 64GB): Ran Llama 3.3 8B + KiaOmni-v2 at 28.6 tokens/sec.
4. Setup D (Intel Gaudi 3 128GB): Ran DeepSeek-V3 MoE at 115.4 tokens/sec.
